Don’t release the item yet

A notification saying a payment arrived doesn’t confirm the money reached your account.

From Messages · Today, 14:32

What to do

  1. 1 Open your own banking app yourself. Don’t use a link or number from the message.
  2. 2 Check the money shows in your available balance, not just as pending.
  3. 3 Only then hand over the item.

What we noticed

Pressure to hand over goods A fake payment message followed by “release the item now” is a common marketplace scam. Banks don’t tell you to release goods.
Claims a payment was made A notification can say anything. Only your banking app shows what actually arrived.
Came as a message, not from a bank app Anyone can send a message that looks like a bank alert.
