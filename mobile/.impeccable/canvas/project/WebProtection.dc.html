Web protection

Block scam sites in every app

Web protection uses Android’s VPN feature, entirely on this phone. Here’s exactly what that means.

What it sees The names of sites that apps look up, like example.com. Not the pages, not what you type, and no other traffic.
What it does Stops known scam sites from loading, and warns you about sites pretending to be a bank or payment service.
What’s kept Only the sites it blocked or warned about, on this phone, for 7 days. Nothing else is recorded or sent to Authentik.
Where lookups go On to your network’s usual DNS server, as they would without Authentik. If there isn’t one, to Cloudflare or Google.
Good to know It doesn’t hide your location or encrypt your traffic. Android allows one VPN at a time, so this replaces any other VPN you use.

Android will ask you to allow the connection next.

Apps or browsers that use their own secure DNS, or Private DNS set to a provider.

New scam sites that aren’t on the list yet. A site that isn’t blocked isn’t proven safe.
