Let Authentik check your notifications

Scammers send fake bank alerts and payment messages. Authentik reads new notifications and warns you before you act on a fake one.

  • Checked on this phone Notification text never leaves your phone, and only the warning is kept.
  • Only what apps show Authentik sees the notification, not your chats or other apps’ screens.
  • You’re in control Turn it off any time, in Authentik or in Android settings.
Turn on in Android settings Not now

Find “Authentik fraud protection” and switch it on. Android shows a warning there. That’s expected.
